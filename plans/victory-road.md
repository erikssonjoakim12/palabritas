# After the 8th medal: Waterfall, the way to Victory Road, a legend (the user, 2026-11-10)

Ideas to plan in detail later. Not built yet.

## Waterfall (CASCADA)

- A field move from the **8th medal** (`FIELD_MOVES`, like Surf, Cut and Strength): swimming, face a waterfall (`!`)
  and press A (or CASCADA in the team menu) to climb it. Palabritas that can really do it (the big swimmers: Espadón,
  Lagartón, Mordiscón...: to choose).
- The waterfall tile is already in the game (the Cañón del Guardabarranco's falls).

## The way to Victory Road: by sea, east from Pueblo Amanecer

- Pueblo Amanecer's sea is closed by rocks now (the edge of the world). With the 8 medals, a gap opens in the rocks on
  its east side (or the rocks are only there until then), and you surf east from home: the journey ends where it began.
- Soon along that sea route, **a waterfall** to climb (needs CASCADA): so only after the 8th medal.
- Then Victory Road (Calle Victoria? a cave or a mountain: to decide) and the Elite Four.
- Trainers on the water (swimmers, fishers on rocks), the strongest so far; a Palacentro before Victory Road.

## Above the canyon's waterfall: a legend, or an easter egg

- In the Cañón del Guardabarranco, the waterfall leads up to a place you can only reach with CASCADA.
- Up there: **a legendary Palabrita** (from a Nicaraguan legend, like the Cadejos: plans/cadejos.md), or an easter egg.
  Ideas to choose from: *Chico Largo* (the guardian of the Charco Verde lagoon on Ometepe, who makes deals with
  people), *La Mocuana* (the woman in the cave at Sébaco), the *Güegüense* (the play's wise old trickster: an easter
  egg more than a battle).
