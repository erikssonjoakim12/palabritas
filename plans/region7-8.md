## Town 7: Puerto Rayo, on a stormy lake (gym 7, Eléctrico)

  Granada), the town on the biggest ones, joined by wooden bridges and piers. Outer islands by Surf; Fly lands at

- **The gym**: Eléctrico, 4 Palabritas, top level 50 (Fragatón, Garrobón, Dantón, and a Tigrillo); three trainers.

## Town 8: Ciudad Ceniza, the old capital in the lava (gym 8, Dragón)

- On Ruta 9's grass and the river's land below the volcano (the map `ceniza` takes Ruta 9's place: a save there keeps
  its spot). Inspired by León Viejo, Nicaragua's first capital, left after the earthquakes and eruptions of 1610 and
  dug out of the ash centuries later.
- Old streets half-swallowed by **black lava** (basalt rock and boulders, ash paths), a ruined church sticking out
  of the rock, newer houses built on the hardened lava.
- **Getting in needs Strength**: every way in is blocked by boulders you push aside (from Ruta 5's road; from the
  river, a landing with a boulder behind it). Boulders stay where you push them (the save keeps them).
- **The gym**: Dragón, 5 Palabritas, top level 58; route trainers 42–49 in the lava fields around the town; three
  gym trainers at 49–50 (the user: easier trainers, a strong leader). The medal: **Medalla Ceniza**.

## Strength (FUERZA)

- A field move from the 7th medal (`FIELD_MOVES.strength`): Carablanca (it makes stones float), Dantón, Garrobón,
  Petroglifón, Tinajón, the Lucha types, Rugidón, Lagartón, Salamandrón.
- Face a boulder and press A (or FUERZA in the team menu): «¿Usar FUERZA?». Then walking into a boulder pushes it a
  tile, if the tile beyond is free; it lasts until you leave the map, like Gold/Silver.
