## ART REQUIREMENTS

- Maximum 56×56 pixels per sprite. Smaller is fine for smaller creatures.
- True, grid-aligned pixel art with crisp edges, not a pixelated illustration.
- **Use ONLY colors from the palette below. Pick at most 16, and use the exact same colors in both sprites.**
- Simple shading, clean outlines, expressive silhouettes.
- Fully transparent background.
- No anti-aliasing, gradients, blur, or stray pixels.
- Prioritize charm and readability over detail.
